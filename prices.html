How valuation works

Urban Mining Connect provides a simple reference calculation so collectors can understand the approximate value of a captured material lot before approaching registered buyers.

SIMPLE VALUE FORMULA
Weight × Rate = Value
Example: 10 kg × ₹265/kg = ₹2,650
