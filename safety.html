SAFE RECOVERY

Recover material without unsafe processing.

Safety guidance remains a first-class flow for field workers.

🔥 Do not burn cables

Avoid open burning for copper recovery; route cable through an appropriate processor.

🧪 Avoid acid leaching

Do not use improvised acid processes on PCBs or other electronic components.

🔋 Battery caution

Do not puncture or open lithium batteries; isolate damaged packs and use an authorized route.

🫁 Fume protection

Avoid informal desoldering and other processes that can create hazardous fumes.
